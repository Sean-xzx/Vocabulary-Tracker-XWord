XWord Reading Demo

CHAPTER ONE
The Morning

Anna has the ability to learn a little every day. She opens a book and writes a useful word in her notebook.

The quiet library is a good place to read. A short sentence can help her remember a new word.

CHAPTER TWO
The Review

Anna returns to her notebook. She remembers some words and reviews the others.

Learning takes time. Reading, collecting words, and reviewing them become a simple daily habit.
